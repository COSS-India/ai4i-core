# 2. Before You Begin

## Roles & Responsibilities

**Adopter Admin —** the Adopter's representative, for context. They onboard your institution and assign the tier and budget that define what your institution can consume. You operate within those boundaries.

**Institution Admin —** you. Responsible for your own institution: creating the API keys that give your applications access to LLMs, onboarding your institution's users, and tracking your institution's budget and usage against your assigned tier.

**Institution User —** a user onboarded by the Institution Admin, who can sign in and explore available LLMs in the portal.

{% hint style="danger" %}
**DATA RESPONSIBILITY**

Your institution is the data fiduciary for the application(s) you connect to AI Switch. AI Switch operates only as a routing and metering layer — it does not process, store, or retain your application's data. Compliance with the DPDP Act for any personal or sensitive data your application collects, uses, or processes remains your institution's responsibility.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**NOTE**

Your institution account, and any institution users you add, are activated by your Adopter Admin, who shares sign-in credentials once activated.
{% endhint %}

## Information to Keep Ready

Before you begin, gather the following so you're ready to start:

* Your sign-in credentials, shared by your Adopter Admin.
* The applications that need access — you'll create an API key for each, so know its intended use, permissions, and expiry.
* The institution users you'll onboard — their name and email; phone number is optional.

## Readiness Checklist

Confirm the items below before you begin Part II. Your progress is saved automatically.

**Access**

* [ ] The AI Switch link, shared by your Adopter Admin, opens in a supported browser.
* [ ] You have your sign-in credentials.
* [ ] Your institution has been assigned a tier and budget.

**Applications (API Keys)**

* [ ] The applications that need access are identified.
* [ ] For each, you know the API key name (matching the application name), its permissions, and expiry.

**Institution Users**

* [ ] The institution users you'll onboard are identified, along with their name and email (phone number is optional).

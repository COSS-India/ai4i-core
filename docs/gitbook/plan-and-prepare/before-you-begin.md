# 2. Before You Begin

## Roles & Responsibilities

**Tenant Admin** — responsible for your own institution: onboarding the applications that will consume services, creating and managing their API keys, and monitoring your institution's usage and spend against the assigned tier and budget.

**Adopter Admin** — the Adopter's representative, for context. They onboard your institution, and assign the tier and budget that define what your institution can consume. You operate within those boundaries.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**DATA RESPONSIBILITY**

Your institution is the data fiduciary for the application(s) you connect to AI Switch. AI Switch operates only as a routing and metering layer — it does not process, store, or retain your application's data. Compliance with the DPDP Act for any personal or sensitive data your application collects, uses, or processes remains your institution's responsibility.
{% endhint %}

## Information to Keep Ready

Before beginning, gather the following so setup runs without interruption:

* The activation email sent to you when your institution was onboarded — you will use it to set your password and sign in.
* The list of applications your institution will onboard, along with a contact for each.
* For each API key you will create: its intended use, the permissions it needs, and when it should expire.

## Readiness Checklist

Confirm the items below before you begin Part II.

**Access**

* [ ] You have received the activation email sent when your institution was onboarded.
* [ ] The activation link opens in a supported browser.

**Applications**

* [ ] The applications your institution will onboard are identified.
* [ ] For each application, you have a contact — name, email, and phone number.

**API Keys**

* [ ] For each key you plan to create, you know its intended use.
* [ ] The permissions each key needs are decided.
* [ ] The expiry for each key is decided.

{% hint style="info" %}
**NOTE**

If any of the above isn't ready yet — for example, if you don't yet know your assigned tier or budget — check with your Adopter Admin before proceeding. Tiers and budgets are assigned at the platform level and can't be set or changed by a Tenant Admin.
{% endhint %}

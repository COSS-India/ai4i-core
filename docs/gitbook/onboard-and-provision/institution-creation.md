# 1. Institution Creation

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Objective | Understand how your institution gets set up on AI Switch before you can sign in. |
| Role | Adopter Admin (performed on your behalf, before your onboarding begins) |
| Prerequisites | None — this step precedes your involvement. |

Before you can sign in and begin onboarding, your institution must share the following with your Adopter Admin. Your Adopter Admin uses it to create your institution on AI Switch and assign it a Tier and Budget:

## Information to Share with Your Adopter Admin

* Name of the institution.
* Name and email of the contact person who will act as Institution Admin.
* The Tier your institution wants to operate on.
* The Budget within which your institution wants to operate.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Outcome | Your institution is created on AI Switch with its Tier and Budget assigned, defining the consumption ceilings and spend cap your institution and its Applications will operate within. |
| Next | Your Adopter Admin activates your institution account and shares your sign-in credentials. Proceed to [Signing In](signing-in.md). |

# Privacy Policy

*Version 1.0 · Last updated: 18 August 2026*
*Centre for Open Societal Systems (COSS) · IIIT-Bangalore · Bengaluru, India*

## 1. Introduction

AI Switch is an open-source, self-hostable Language AI orchestration platform developed by the Centre for Open Societal Systems ("COSS"), formed at IIIT-Bangalore (IIIT-B) in partnership with EkStep Foundation. COSS is registered at 26/C, IIIT Bangalore, Electronics City Phase 1, Bengaluru – 560100, Karnataka, India.

This Privacy Policy explains how COSS collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data of users of the AI Switch platform ("Platform"), and describes your rights under applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 ("DPDP Act").

By registering on and using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Who This Policy Applies To

This Privacy Policy applies to all individuals who register on and use the Platform, including platform administrators, organisation administrators, and users registered under an organisation's account (collectively referred to as "Users" in this Policy).

This Policy applies to the reference deployment of AI Switch operated by COSS. It does not apply to:

- Self-hosted deployments where organisations deploy AI Switch on their own infrastructure. In those deployments, the organisation is the Data Fiduciary and is responsible for its own Privacy Policy.
- Third-party websites or services linked from the Platform.

## 3. What Personal Data We Collect

### 3.1 Information You Provide at Registration

When you create an account on the Platform, we collect:

- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number (if provided voluntarily)
- Organisation name
- Your assigned role on the Platform

We do not collect Aadhaar numbers, financial information, biometric data, or any information beyond what is listed above.

### 3.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Platform, we automatically record certain technical information to enable the Platform to function securely and reliably, including:

- Your IP address
- Unique identifiers associated with your account and your organisation's account
- The type of service requested, the time of the request, and whether it succeeded or failed

This information is used solely for security monitoring, diagnosing technical issues, and ensuring the Platform operates correctly. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose.

### 3.3 AI Service Requests — What We Do Not Retain

**Audio recordings, text, and images** that you submit to the Platform's Language AI services for processing **are not stored or retained by COSS**. These are processed in real time and discarded once the result is returned. COSS does not have access to, and does not retain, the content of your AI service requests.

## 4. How We Use Your Personal Data

We use your personal data only for the following purposes:

- To create and manage your account on the Platform.
- To verify your identity and authenticate your access.
- To communicate with you about your account, including security alerts and important account-related notifications.
- To diagnose and resolve technical issues.
- To comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use your personal data for advertising, marketing, sale to third parties, or building personal profiles beyond what is necessary for account management.

**Data Minimisation**

We collect only personal data that is adequate, relevant, and limited to what is necessary for the purposes described in this Policy. We do not collect personal data beyond what is required to create and manage your account on the Platform.

**Purpose Limitation**

Personal data collected during registration is processed solely for the specified, explicit, and lawful purpose of creating and managing your account on the Platform. It will not be processed for any purpose beyond what is described in this Policy without your explicit consent or as required by applicable law.

## 5. Legal Basis for Processing

COSS processes your personal data on the basis of your **informed and freely given consent**, which you provide at the time of account registration through an affirmative consent mechanism (see Section 8). Where required by applicable law or for protection of your vital interests, we may process data on that basis instead.

In line with the DPDP Act 2023, COSS is the **Data Fiduciary** for the reference deployment and is responsible for all personal data collected and processed on this deployment.

## 6. AI Service Data — Adopters' Responsibility

AI Switch is designed as an open, self-hostable platform for Language AI services. When organisations ("Adopters") deploy the Platform on their own infrastructure, they are the Data Fiduciaries for the personal data of their own end users.

Adopters are responsible for:

- Informing their end users that their audio, text, or other content is being submitted to AI services for processing.
- Obtaining the appropriate consent from their end users under applicable law.
- Complying with all applicable data protection and privacy laws in their jurisdiction.

COSS does not store AI service request content and has no visibility into the personal data of an Adopter's end users.

## 7. Who Has Access to Your Data

Your registration data is accessible to:

- COSS, as the operator of the reference deployment, for the purpose of operating, maintaining, and securing the Platform.
- The administrator of your organisation's account on the Platform, for the purpose of managing your access.

Your data is not shared with third parties for commercial purposes. We may disclose your data only where required by a court order, statutory obligation, or at the direction of a competent government authority under applicable Indian law.

## 8. Consent

Account registration requires your affirmative consent, given by checking a mandatory checkbox at the time of registration:

> "I agree to AI Switch's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy."

The checkbox is unchecked by default and your account cannot be created unless you actively check it. You may withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account (see Section 10).

## 9. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active on the Platform. When you delete your account, your personal identifiers are pseudonymised and your access to the platform is revoked. Certain account and usage records are retained for audit and legal compliance purposes.

To request permanent erasure of your personal data, please contact us at info@ai4inclusion.org. We will respond within 30 days.

## 10. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act 2023 and applicable Indian law, you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

**Right to Access Information**

You have the right to know what personal data we hold about you and how it is used, as described in this Policy.

**Right to Correction**

You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data at any time via your account profile settings, or by contacting us at info@ai4inclusion.org.

**Right to Erasure**

You may delete your account at any time via Account Settings → Delete Account. Once deleted, your personal identifiers are pseudonymised and your access to the Platform is revoked. To request permanent erasure of all your personal data from our systems, contact us at info@ai4inclusion.org. We will acknowledge your request within 48 hours and respond within 30 days.

**Right to Withdraw Consent**

You may withdraw your consent to the processing of your personal data at any time by deleting your account. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

**Right to Grievance Redressal**

If you have a concern about how we handle your personal data, you may contact our Grievance Officer at info@ai4inclusion.org. We will acknowledge your grievance within 48 hours and aim to resolve it within 30 days.

**Right to Nominate**

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you may nominate another individual to exercise your data rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity.

## 11. Data Security

COSS implements appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, accidental loss, and misuse. These include access controls, encryption of credentials, role-based permissions, and data isolation between organisations.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we take reasonable precautions, we cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

These measures comply with the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules 2011 and are aligned with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.

## 12. Data Storage Location

Personal data of users of the reference deployment is stored on servers located in India, in compliance with applicable Indian data localisation requirements.

## 13. Children

AI Switch is an enterprise platform designed for use by organisations, government bodies, and research institutions. It is not directed at individuals under 18 years of age.

Adopters who deploy AI Switch for use cases involving minors are responsible for obtaining verifiable parental consent in accordance with the DPDP Act 2023 and applicable law.

## 14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will publish a notice on the Platform at least 30 days before the changes take effect. For significant changes that affect how we collect or use your personal data, we will seek fresh consent from registered users before the changes take effect.
